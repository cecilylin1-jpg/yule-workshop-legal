This policy describes the Android app Tarot Card Reading: AI Tarot (Chinese title: 塔羅牌占卜：AI 塔羅解牌). It is the policy to host and paste into Google Play.

Readings in this app are generated by artificial intelligence. They are for entertainment and self-reflection only. The app is not a human fortune teller, psychic, or counselor, and it does not give medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

Who we are

The publisher is 予樂工坊 (YU LE WORKSHOP). For privacy questions, deletion requests, or other requests about your data, email cecilylin1@gmail.com.

What data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, the following may be processed:

  • Questions you type for a reading or a follow-up, if you enter one. Please do not include your name, address, phone number, email, ID numbers, or medical, financial, or legal details.
  • Card draws and reading context: card identifiers, whether a card is reversed, the topic you chose (for example love, work, or money), language, and related reading identifiers.
  • Daily card and optional zodiac sign. Your sign, if you choose one, is stored on the device and is not required.
  • Check-in and streak data used to record consecutive daily check-ins and any reward earned for a streak (for example a free deep reading after seven days).
  • Purchase and entitlement records: product identifiers, purchase status, a hashed purchase token, price tier (such as first unlock versus later unlocks), subscription status, and whether a follow-up or premium love report was granted. We do not receive your card number or bank details.
  • Device and advertising identifiers when ads or a consent form are used, including the Android advertising ID and other identifiers described in Google’s Mobile Ads disclosure, as well as an app install or account identifier used to rate-limit requests and attach entitlements.
  • Preferences and history on the device: language, chosen spread, recent readings and follow-ups, and similar settings.
  • Technical data inherent to a network request to our servers or to Google, such as IP address, time, and basic device or app version information needed to deliver, secure, and debug the service.

We do not ask for your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, SMS, or date of birth. We do not currently operate our own analytics or crash-reporting product.

AI readings

When you request a full reading, the app sends the card draw, topic, language, and any question you typed to our backend. The backend is a proxy: it forwards that request to an AI model and returns the generated text to the app. Model API keys are kept on the server; they are not stored in the app.

  • Readings are AI-generated and labeled as such. No person at 予樂工坊 reads your question as a psychic or fortune teller in order to produce the reading.
  • We use this information only to generate and deliver the reading, to enforce purchase and safety rules, and to prevent abuse. We do not sell your questions or readings, and we do not use the text of your questions for advertising or ad targeting.
  • By default, the text of your questions and generated readings is stored on your device. Our servers process a request long enough to produce the reading, and may keep a transient copy for up to 24 hours for delivery, security, or troubleshooting. We do not keep a long-term archive of prompt text as a default practice. If we later offer optional cloud sync or a signed-in history, that copy will be opt-in, kept until you delete it or close the account, and described in an updated policy.
  • We contract with the model provider so that, by default, prompts are not used to train their models. If that arrangement changes, we will update this policy.
  • Requests are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection. You should still avoid entering information you would not want processed by an automated service.

Local storage

Preferences, the daily card state, recent reading text, and similar journal content are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes that local data. Files or screenshots you save elsewhere stay where you put them. Local storage alone cannot restore a purchase or a streak after you uninstall; restored entitlements depend on Google Play and, when enabled, a server-side record linked to a stable purchase identity.

Advertising and consent (Google AdMob)

In storefronts where the free experience includes ads, the app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including interstitial ads and rewarded videos. In some storefronts the app may not request ads. The Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) libraries may still be present in the app.

Where Google requires a consent or privacy choice, including the EEA, the UK, and regulated US states, UMP asks for that choice before ads are requested. The app is not directed at children, and the consent request is not tagged as a child’s request. Where a privacy-options entry is required, the app provides a way to open Google’s form again. If you do not consent where consent is required, ads are not requested; the app does not bypass the consent form.

Google’s disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK says the SDK collects and shares the following, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit with TLS:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location. The app does not ask for precise location and does not use GPS.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash or performance data collected by the SDK.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts already signed in on the device.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Subscribers are not shown ads in the app’s own ad placements. Google’s policies describe how Google keeps and controls advertising data: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads. See also Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure.

If we later add mediation partners besides Google, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form so they match the SDKs actually shipped.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

One-time unlocks, follow-up questions, premium love reports, and subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. The app and our servers do not receive your card number, bank details, or full payment instrument. The price you see at checkout is the price Google Play returns for your country.

After a purchase or a restore, we may receive a purchase token and status from Google so we can verify the purchase, grant the entitlement, prevent reuse of the same token, apply the correct price tier, and restore purchases. We store a hash of the token and the resulting entitlement or check-in state, not the raw token in ordinary logs. Refunds, cancellations, and subscription management are handled in Google Play. Subscription terms, price, billing period, auto-renewal, and how to cancel are shown before you complete a purchase, and you can manage or cancel in Google Play.

Premium love reports and paid follow-up questions are sold separately, including for subscribers, unless a specific earned reward (such as a 7-day check-in voucher) says otherwise in the app.

If you are under 18 (or under the age of majority where you live), use the app only with a parent or legal guardian’s permission.
