Effective 10 November 2026. Package name tw.yule.bedtimestories. Publisher: 予樂工坊 (YU LE WORKSHOP).

This policy describes the Android app Bedtime Stories: Voice Clone — Kids stories read in Mom & Dad’s voice (睡前故事：AI聲音克隆 爸爸媽媽說故事). It is the policy to host and paste into Google Play. It matches the app as built: on-device voice cloning, a fixed story library, optional download of public model files, and no publisher account, advertising, or analytics SDK.

Who we are

The publisher is 予樂工坊 (YU LE WORKSHOP). The app does not ask you to create an account with us. There is no publisher login, password, or profile, and no cross-device sync of your voice or library.

What the App does

A parent records or imports their own adult voice. The app builds a voice profile on the device and uses it to read our fixed library of public-domain and original-rewrite children’s stories. The store listing and in-app screens disclose AI-generated speech. The app does not offer user-generated stories or voice sharing.

Voice samples and voiceprints

If you choose to clone a voice, the app collects and stores the following on the device, for the sole purpose of reading stories in that voice:

  • Voice samples. A short recording you make in the app (microphone) or a WAV file you import. The app copies an imported file into its private storage. Your original file stays where you stored it.
  • Voiceprint (speaker embedding / voice profile). Numeric parameters derived from your sample, stored as a local voice profile and used only to synthesize story audio in that voice.
  • Generated story audio and working files. Cached narrations and temporary paragraph files so the same voice and story can be replayed without generating again.
  • Consent record. The checkboxes you accept, the policy version, a UTC timestamp, and whether any optional server upload was allowed. A copy of this record is also stored with the local voice profile.

These files can identify you. They stay in the app’s private storage on your phone. The current version does not encrypt them at rest. Do not use sensitive recordings. You can delete them in Settings at any time, as described below.

How voice is processed

Recording, cloning, and synthesis run on your device using an open-source model whose license permits commercial use. Your voice sample and voiceprint are not sent to us and are not sent to a third-party cloud text-to-speech API.

The app includes unused code for an optional HTTPS self-hosted server, and a Settings field for a URL. We have not designated or begun operating any such host. No voice is uploaded unless a host is configured and you give separate upload consent. If we later operate or designate a server of our own, we will update this policy and ask for fresh consent before any upload.

You may clone only your own adult voice — not a child’s, a celebrity’s, or anyone else’s.

Other information stored on the device

The app also keeps, locally only:

  • Language preference, favourited story IDs, and which stories have been generated (used for the free-tier quota).
  • Whether the free clone quota has been used, and local paths or IDs for the current voice and recording.
  • Optional on-device timing logs from model stages. Those logs are written so they do not include the recording, the embedding, or story text.
  • If you use Report inappropriate content, a draft is saved on the device only. The current version does not send reports to us. Please do not put personal information in a draft.
  • If you install a model pack, the published model files are stored in the app’s private storage so cloning can run offline.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or Google account identifier in the app. We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting product, and the app does not include advertising or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

  • Microphone is requested only when you start a recording, so the app can capture your own voice sample. You can refuse or later revoke it in Android settings. Listening to stories does not need the microphone.
  • Internet is used to download the public model pack when you ask the app to, and would be used only if a server were later configured as described above. Cached story audio can be replayed offline.
  • Wake lock is used so playback can continue during a sleep timer. The app does not currently run a foreground media service.
  • File import uses Android’s system file picker. The app does not request broad access to your shared storage. Only a file you pick is copied into app-private storage.

Model downloads

The on-device models are large published files (on the order of hundreds of megabytes). When a public download address is configured, the app fetches checksummed pieces from a GitHub Releases host over HTTPS, and can resume an interrupted download. That transfer is of public model files, not your voice, voiceprint, or stories. GitHub may see technical data such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement. You can instead import a model ZIP through the system file picker with no download. If no public address is configured, only offline import is available.

Purchases and Google Play Billing

Paid plans, when offered, will be sold through Google Play Billing (including subscriptions and a one-time lifetime option, as then shown in the app). Google Play processes the payment. We do not receive your card number, bank details, or other payment-instrument data. Final prices are those Google Play displays for your account and country.

The current version shows illustrative proposed prices and does not process purchases, restore purchases, or sign you in to claim a subscription. Local free-tier counters (one clone and three stories in this version) are stored on the device and can reset if you reinstall. When billing is connected, we will keep this description aligned with the live products.

Children’s privacy

Voice cloning, consent, recording, import, server settings, and deletion are intended for a parent or other adult. An arithmetic parental gate is shown before those adult flows. The app itself tells you that this gate, and the consent checkboxes, are not verifiable parental consent and are not identity or age verification.

We do not treat a child as the account holder, and we do not collect personal information from children as a data subject. Children may listen to stories that a parent has generated. Do not record, import, or clone a child’s voice. This policy is written with Google Play Families requirements and the US COPPA rule against collecting personal information from children under 13 in mind. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also contact us about access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data that we actually hold — in the current version, voice and related data remain on your device unless you later consent to a disclosed server.

Consent

Before cloning, you must confirm that you are recording only your own voice and consent to story narration, that you are at least 18, and that you will not clone a child’s, a celebrity’s, or another person’s voice. If a server were configured, a separate upload consent would also be required. Changing a server setting clears the saved consent so it must be given again. Withdrawing consent, or deleting your voice, stops further cloning and narration with that voice.

Retention

  • Voice samples, voiceprints, generated audio, consent records, and related local files are kept until you delete the voice or the local account data, or until you uninstall the app.
  • The current version does not automatically delete a raw recording after a successful clone, and it does not apply a timed (for example seven-day) auto-delete.
  • Android backup of the app’s private data is turned off in the app manifest. Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage, including models, recordings, and local preferences. Files you kept outside the app, such as an original imported WAV, are not removed.

Your rights and deletion

In Settings you may:

  • Delete voice & audio — removes the local recording, voice profile, generated and cached audio, consent record, and import temporaries. If a remote voice ever existed, the app tries to delete it on that server first and keeps local identifiers if that deletion is not confirmed.
  • Delete local account data — does the above and also clears other local app data (including model files, preferences, quotas, and unsent report drafts). There is no remote publisher account to close.

You can also write to us at the address below. Deletion cannot be undone.

Third parties and sharing

  • Google Play — distribution of the app, and payment processing when billing is connected, under Google’s terms and privacy policy.
  • GitHub — only as the public host of model-pack files you choose to download, as described above.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your voice sample or voiceprint with advertisers or a third-party speech API. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, but the current version does not send your voice data to us.

Security

Network transfers the app makes use HTTPS. Cleartext traffic is disabled in the release app. Voice data is stored in app-private storage, and Android backup of that storage is disabled. As noted above, the current version does not add its own encryption at rest. No method is perfectly secure; we take reasonable measures and will describe any future server access controls in an updated policy before that path is used.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change and the updated page will be published at this same address. We will also notify in the app where practical. A material change to how voice is processed may require fresh consent.

生效日：2026年11月10日。套件名稱 tw.yule.bedtimestories。發布者：予樂工坊（YU LE WORKSHOP）。

本政策說明 Android 應用程式「睡前故事：AI聲音克隆 爸爸媽媽說故事」（Bedtime Stories: Voice Clone — Kids stories read in Mom & Dad’s voice）。這是準備放上網站並貼到 Google Play 的版本，內容對應目前的應用程式：裝置端語音克隆、固定故事庫、可選的公開模型檔下載；沒有向我們註冊的帳號，也沒有廣告或分析 SDK。

我們是誰

發布者是予樂工坊（YU LE WORKSHOP）。應用程式不會要求你向我們註冊帳號，沒有發布者登入、密碼或個人檔案，也不會把你的聲音或書庫同步到其他裝置。

本 App 做什麼

家長錄製或匯入本人的成人聲音。應用程式在裝置上建立聲音檔案，並以該聲音朗讀我們提供的固定公版／原創改寫兒童故事。商店頁面與應用程式內會標示「含 AI 生成語音」。本 App 不提供使用者自製故事，也不提供聲音分享。

語音樣本與聲紋

若你選擇建立克隆聲音，應用程式會在裝置上蒐集並儲存下列資料，目的僅限以該聲音朗讀故事：

  • 語音樣本。你在應用程式內錄製的短錄音（麥克風），或你匯入的 WAV 檔。匯入時，應用程式會把副本存進其私有空間；你原本的檔案仍留在你存放的位置。
  • 聲紋（說話者嵌入／聲音模型參數）。由樣本計算出的數值參數，以本機聲音檔案保存，只用來合成該聲音的故事音訊。
  • 生成的故事音檔與工作檔。快取的朗讀與暫時的段落檔，讓同一聲音、同一故事不必重複生成即可重播。
  • 同意存證。你勾選的項目、政策版本、UTC 時間戳，以及是否另行允許上傳至伺服器。此紀錄也會一併存在本機聲音檔案中。

這些檔案可能識別你，並存放在手機上的應用程式私有空間。目前版本尚未提供靜態加密，請勿使用敏感錄音。你可以隨時在「設定」中依下文所述刪除。

語音如何處理

錄音、克隆與合成都在你的裝置上完成，使用允許商業使用的開源模型。你的語音樣本與聲紋不會傳給我們，也不會送到第三方雲端文字轉語音 API。

應用程式內含尚未使用的選用 HTTPS 自架伺服器程式碼，以及設定頁中的網址欄位。我們尚未指定或開始營運任何此類主機。除非已設定主機且你另外同意上傳，否則不會上傳聲音。若日後由我們營運或指定自有伺服器，我們會更新本政策，並在任何上傳前再次取得同意。

僅允許克隆你本人的成人聲音，禁止克隆兒童、名人或他人聲音。

其他存在裝置上的資料

應用程式也只在本機保存：

  • 語言偏好、收藏的故事編號，以及已生成的故事編號（用於免費額度）。
  • 免費克隆額度是否已使用，以及目前聲音與錄音的本機路徑或識別碼。
  • 可選的本機階段計時紀錄。這些紀錄的寫入方式不包含錄音、聲紋或故事正文。
  • 若你使用「回報不當內容」，草稿只存在裝置上。目前版本不會把回報送給我們。請不要在草稿中填寫個人資料。
  • 若你安裝模型包，公開的模型檔會存在應用程式私有空間，以便離線克隆。

我們不會在應用程式中蒐集你的姓名、電子郵件、電話或 Google 帳戶識別碼。我們沒有自己的分析或當機回報產品，應用程式也不包含廣告或追蹤 SDK。

權限

  • 麥克風只在你按下開始錄音時才會請求，用來擷取你本人的語音樣本。你可以拒絕，或之後在 Android 設定中撤回。只聽故事不需要麥克風。
  • 網路用於在你要求時下載公開模型包；若日後依上文設定了伺服器，也會用到網路。已快取的故事音檔可以離線重播。
  • 保持喚醒（Wake lock）用於睡眠計時器期間繼續播放。目前版本尚未實作前景媒體服務。
  • 檔案匯入透過 Android 系統檔案選擇器完成。應用程式不會要求廣泛存取你的共用儲存空間，只會把你選取的檔案複製到應用程式私有空間。

模型下載

裝置端模型是較大的公開檔案（約數百 MB）。若已設定公開下載網址，應用程式會以 HTTPS 從 GitHub Releases 主機下載經過校驗的分塊，並可續傳中斷的下載。此傳輸是公開模型檔，不是你的聲音、聲紋或故事。GitHub 可能依其隱私權聲明看到 IP 位址等技術資料。你也可以改用系統檔案選擇器匯入模型 ZIP，無須下載。若尚未設定公開網址，則僅能離線匯入。

兒童隱私

語音克隆、同意、錄音、匯入、伺服器設定與刪除，是給家長或其他成年人使用的。進入這些流程前會出現算術家長閘門。應用程式會說明：此閘門與同意勾選不是可驗證的家長同意，也不是身分或年齡驗證。

我們不以兒童作為帳號主體，也不向兒童蒐集其作為資料當事人的個人資料。兒童可以收聽家長已生成的故事。請勿錄製、匯入或克隆兒童聲音。本政策撰寫時已考量 Google Play 家庭政策，以及美國 COPPA 關於不得向未滿 13 歲兒童蒐集個人資料的規定。若你在歐洲經濟區或英國，也可以就我們實際持有的個人資料，向我們請求近用、更正、刪除或限制處理——以目前版本而言，語音及相關資料留在你的裝置上，除非你日後同意使用已揭露的伺服器。

同意

開始克隆前，你必須確認只錄製本人聲音並同意用於故事朗讀、你已年滿 18 歲，以及不會克隆兒童、名人或他人聲音。若設定了伺服器，還須另外同意上傳。更改伺服器設定會清除已儲存的同意，必須重新同意。撤回同意或刪除聲音後，即無法再以該聲音繼續克隆或朗讀。

保留期間
