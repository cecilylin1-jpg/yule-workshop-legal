Effective 10 November 2026. Package name com.yule.spreadsheeteditor. Publisher: 予樂工坊 (YU LE WORKSHOP).

This policy describes the Android app Spreadsheet Editor: XLSX. It is the policy to host and paste into Google Play. It matches the app as shipped: a local editor, Google advertising and consent services, and a one-time purchase handled by Google Play.

Who we are

The publisher is 予樂工坊 (YU LE WORKSHOP). The app does not ask you to create an account with us. There is no password, profile, or publisher login.

Workbooks stay on the device

The app opens, edits, and saves .xlsx workbooks through Android’s file picker and through files other apps share with it. Workbook contents are read and written on the device. We do not run a server that receives those files, and we do not send cell contents to advertising or billing services.

  • Recent names and locations are stored on the device so the home screen can list them.
  • The first time you overwrite a file you opened, a private backup of the original is stored in the app’s storage on the device.
  • A shared file opens read-only until you save a copy.
  • If you pick a cloud folder yourself, that folder’s provider (for example the one you already use) handles the file under its own terms. That transfer is not made to us.

Uninstalling the app removes the app’s private storage, including backups and the local purchase flag. Files you saved through the system picker stay where you put them.

Advertising and consent

The free app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including interstitial ads and rewarded videos. The User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for a consent or privacy choice where Google requires one, including the EEA, the UK, and regulated US states. The app is not directed at children, and the consent request is not tagged as a child’s request.

Google’s disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK says the SDK collects and shares the following, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit with TLS:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location. The app does not ask for precise location and does not use GPS.
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts already signed in on the device.

The ads libraries also merge permissions for the advertising ID and for Privacy Sandbox ad services (ad ID, attribution, and topics). Those permissions belong to the ads SDK. We do not read your contacts, photos, or messages for advertising.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Where a privacy-options control is required, the app offers Ad choices, which opens Google’s form. Buying Remove ads stops the app from requesting ads. Interstitials are not shown on first launch or while you are editing. A rewarded video can pause interstitials for 30 minutes. If the network is unavailable, ads do not load and editing still works.

Google’s own policy describes how long Google keeps advertising data and how to control it: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time product sold by Google Play, not a subscription. Google Play processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number or bank details. The button shows the price Google Play returns.

After a purchase or a restore, the app stores a local flag on the device: the product id, whether it is owned, and a time. It does not send that record to a server of ours. This version does not add a separate server check of the receipt. Refunds and purchase state are handled by Google Play. Restore purchases asks Play for the same Google account’s purchases.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number collected by us.
  • No workbook contents sent to us or to the ad and purchase SDKs as file data.
  • No precise location, contacts, SMS, microphone, or camera.
  • No sale of personal information by the publisher. We do not run our own analytics or crash product.

Children

The app is not made for children. Do not use it for a child. It shows ads and offers a purchase. The target audience declared to Google Play is not under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change and the updated page will be published at this same address.

生效日：2026年11月10日。套件名稱 com.yule.spreadsheeteditor。發布者：予樂工坊（YU LE WORKSHOP）。

本政策說明 Android 應用程式「表格編輯器：試算表XLSX」。這是準備放上網站並貼到 Google Play 的版本，內容對應目前的應用程式：本機編輯、Google 廣告與同意聲明服務，以及由 Google Play 處理的一次性購買。

我們是誰

發布者是予樂工坊（YU LE WORKSHOP）。應用程式不會要求你向我們註冊帳號，沒有密碼、個人檔案或發布者登入。

活頁簿留在裝置上

應用程式透過 Android 的檔案選擇器，以及透過其他應用程式分享進來的檔案，開啟、修改並儲存 .xlsx 活頁簿。活頁簿內容在裝置上讀取與寫入。我們沒有接收這些檔案的伺服器，也不會把儲存格內容送給廣告或購買服務。

  • 最近使用的檔名與位置存在這台裝置上，用來在首頁列出。
  • 第一次覆寫你開啟的檔案時，原始檔的私人備份會存在應用程式在這台裝置上的儲存空間。
  • 分享進來的檔案先以唯讀開啟，直到你另存新檔。
  • 若你自己選了雲端資料夾，該資料夾的提供者（例如你原本就在用的服務）依其條款處理檔案。那不是傳給我們。

解除安裝會移除應用程式的私人儲存，包括備份與本機的購買紀錄。你透過系統選擇器存到其他位置的檔案會留在那裡。

廣告與同意

免費版透過 Google 行動廣告 SDK（AdMob）顯示廣告，包含插頁廣告與獎勵短片。在 Google 要求的地區（包括歐洲經濟區、英國，以及受規範的美國州），User Messaging Platform（UMP）會詢問同意或隱私選擇。本應用程式不以兒童為對象，同意請求也不會標記為兒童的請求。

Google 對行動廣告 SDK 的說明指出，該 SDK 會為了廣告、分析與防弊而收集並分享下列資料，傳輸時以 TLS 加密：

  • IP 位址，可能用來估計大致位置。應用程式不要求精確位置，也不使用 GPS。
  • 產品互動，例如啟動應用程式、點按與影片觀看。
  • 診斷資訊，例如啟動時間、無回應比例與耗電。
  • 裝置與帳戶識別碼：Android 廣告 ID、app set ID，以及在適用時與裝置上已登入帳戶相關的識別碼。

廣告程式庫也會併入廣告 ID，以及 Privacy Sandbox 廣告服務（廣告 ID、歸因與 topics）的權限。這些權限屬於廣告 SDK。我們不會為了廣告讀取你的聯絡人、相片或訊息。

你可以在 Android 設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID。在需要提供隱私選項的地區，應用程式有「廣告選擇」，會開啟 Google 的表單。購買「移除廣告」後，應用程式不再請求廣告。插頁廣告不會在第一次開啟時出現，也不會在編輯途中出現。獎勵短片可以讓插頁廣告暫停 30 分鐘。沒有網路時廣告不會載入，編輯仍然可用。

Google 如何保存與控管廣告資料，見 policies.google.com/privacy 與 policies.google.com/technologies/ads。

購買

「移除廣告」是 Google Play 銷售的一次性商品，不是訂閱。付款由 Google Play 處理。應用程式不會收到你的卡號或銀行資料。按鈕顯示 Google Play 回傳的價格。

購買或還原之後，應用程式只在裝置上記下：商品編號、是否已擁有，以及時間。這筆記錄不會送到我們的伺服器。這個版本沒有另外的伺服器收據檢查。退款與購買狀態由 Google Play 處理。「還原購買」會向 Play 查詢同一個 Google 帳戶的購買。

我們不收集的資料

  • 我們不收集帳號、姓名、電子郵件或電話。
  • 活頁簿內容不會當成檔案資料送給我們或廣告與購買 SDK。
  • 沒有精確位置、聯絡人、簡訊、麥克風或相機。
  • 發布者不出售個人資料。我們沒有自己的分析或當機收集產品。

兒童

本應用程式不是為兒童製作的，請不要給兒童使用。它會顯示廣告並提供購買。向 Google Play 宣告的目標對象不包含 13 歲以下。
